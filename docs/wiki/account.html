Your account

![The sign-in screen — the inner of the two doors](../images/login.png)

Signing in

Accounts are made by the owner — there is no self-registration; this portal is a guest list, not a website. Depending on how the portal is published you may pass two doors: an email code at the outer door (Cloudflare Access) and your GameKeepr username and password at the inner one. They are separate on purpose: the outer door keeps the internet out, the inner one knows who you are.

Your first password

A new account comes with a one-time password. It works exactly once: you sign in and must replace it before anything else works. Until then the account can do nothing, so a temporary password in a chat message is not a standing risk.

Changing your password

Your name in the top bar → change password. You need your current one. Forgotten it? The owner can reset it, which hands you a new one-time password and signs out your other sessions.

Sessions

Signing in lasts for a month per browser. The owner can see which sessions exist (browser and rough location, for spotting something that should not be there) and can end them.

GameKeepr is open source. Source, issues and releases on GitHub.
